PHASMORA 1.0.0

Stereo VST3 audio memory effect for Windows x64.
Close your DAW before installing or uninstalling.

The complete PHASMORA.vst3 bundle is installed into the standard Common Files\VST3
location. Only PHASMORA-owned files are installed and removed.
