PHASMORA 1.0.0 - Windows x64 VST3

Requires Windows 10 or later and a 64-bit VST3-compatible audio host.
Close your DAW before installing or upgrading. Back up your projects.

Recommended: run PHASMORA-1.0.0-Windows-Setup.exe from the official release.
The installer is unsigned; Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning.

Manual installation:
1. Extract the ZIP completely.
2. Copy the ENTIRE PHASMORA.vst3 directory, including Contents and all files,
   to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\ (administrator permission needed).
3. Rescan VST3 plugins in your DAW and open PHASMORA as an audio effect.
Do not copy just the inner binary. Manual uninstall: remove that bundle only.

Previous GHOST users: use the installer for verified migration. Do not leave
GHOST.vst3 and PHASMORA.vst3 together in scan paths: they share plugin IDs.
The installer refuses unknown/modified GHOST bundles and unexpected files.
Manual ZIP installation does NOT run migration or remove an old GHOST bundle.

Frozen audio is not saved in sessions; recapture it after reopening. Old FL
Studio project substitution, clean-machine admin install, physical HiDPI and
Smart Disable have not been manually verified for this release.
